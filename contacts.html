🤖 How to Receive Instant Telegram Alerts

  1. Install Telegram on the caregiver or family member's phone.
  2. Open Telegram and search for @userinfobot, then tap Start.
  3. The bot replies with their numeric Id (e.g., 583920194).
  4. Copy that ID and paste it into the Telegram Chat ID field above.
  5. ElderSOS will deliver real-time emergency SOS messages with live GPS map pins straight to their phone!
